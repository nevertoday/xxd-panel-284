## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one miniature monument inside a stable frame, not a science-fiction illustration. Place the subject in a circle, shield or square. A few supporting signs, such as an orbit, horizon or sun arc, may serve it. Use halftone, ink gain and a cold-warm print collision of deep blue against sun yellow, warm red or orange. Type is compact institutional sans. Reject a full scene, 3D, cyberpunk, an app logo and a cluttered souvenir badge.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-284/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
